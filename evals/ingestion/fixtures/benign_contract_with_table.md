# FACILITIES MAINTENANCE AGREEMENT

THIS FACILITIES MAINTENANCE AGREEMENT is signed on 2 February 2026 between
GREYSTONE ESTATES MANAGEMENT LIMITED and CALDER & SONS FACILITIES LLP.

## 1. SCOPE OF WORKS

1.1 The Contractor shall maintain the common areas of Greystone House
according to the service levels in Schedule 1.

## 2. CHARGES

2.1 The Employer shall pay the monthly charge of GBP 12,500 within
fourteen (14) days of invoice.

## 3. PERFORMANCE TABLE

The service levels referred to in clause 1.1 are:

| Service level | Target | Remedy threshold |
|---------------|--------|------------------|
| Reactive repairs | 48 hours | 3 breaches in 90 days |
| Planned maintenance | 95% on schedule | 2 missed cycles |
| Cleaning audits | 85% score | 2 consecutive failures |

## 4. TERMINATION

4.1 Either party may terminate on ninety (90) days written notice.

## 5. GOVERNING LAW

5.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
