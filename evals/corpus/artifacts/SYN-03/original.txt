ADDENDUM NO. 1 TO WORKS CONTRACT NO. SYN-02-WC-A
(FIT-OUT OF UNIT 401, MERIDIAN HOUSE — TENANT A: ACME ANALYTICS PRIVATE LIMITED)
================================================================================

THIS ADDENDUM NO. 1 is executed at Mumbai on 15 May 2026 BETWEEN:

(1) MERIDIAN REALTY DEVELOPERS PRIVATE LIMITED (the "Employer"); and

(2) SHARDA CONSTRUCTIONS LLP (the "Contractor").

Reference is made to the Works Contract No. SYN-02-WC-A between the parties
dated 12 January 2026 (the "Principal Contract"). Capitalised expressions used
but not defined in this Addendum bear the meaning given in the Principal
Contract.

NOW IT IS HEREBY AGREED as follows:

1. EXTENSION OF COMPLETION DATE
1.1 The parties agree that the Completion Date under Clause 1.2 of the
    Principal Contract is extended by 30 (thirty) days, from 30 June 2026 to
    30 July 2026, on account of the delay in grant of the building
    association's no objection described in Employer's letter dated
    02 May 2026.
1.2 The extension granted under Clause 1.1 is the Contractor's sole remedy
    for the delay described in Clause 1.1, and no claims for prolongation
    cost shall arise from it.

2. ADDITIONAL WORKS AND PRICE ADJUSTMENT
2.1 The Scope of Works is enlarged to include the sound-rated partitions
    described in Additional Drawing A-101. The Contract Price is increased
    by Rs. 8,50,000 (Rupees eight lakh fifty thousand only), inclusive of
    GST.
2.2 The payment machinery of Clause 4 of the Principal Contract applies to
    the increased Contract Price.

3. LIQUIDATED DAMAGES
3.1 The liquidated damages rate under Clause 5.1 of the Principal Contract
    (0.5% of the Contract Price per completed week of delay, capped at 5% of
    the Contract Price) is unaffected by this Addendum and continues to
    apply, calculated on the extended Completion Date of 30 July 2026.

4. NO OTHER AMENDMENT
4.1 Save as expressly varied by this Addendum, the Principal Contract
    continues in full force and effect unamended.
