PART 1 — BASE WORKS CONTRACT (Tenant A)
WORKS CONTRACT NO. SYN-02-WC-A (FIT-OUT OF UNIT 401, MERIDIAN HOUSE)
================================================================================

THIS WORKS CONTRACT is executed at Mumbai on 12 January 2026 BETWEEN:

(1) MERIDIAN REALTY DEVELOPERS PRIVATE LIMITED, a company incorporated under
the Companies Act, 2013 (CIN U70100MH2012PTC000001), having its registered
office at Meridian House, Bandra Kurla Complex, Mumbai 400051 (the
"Employer"); and

(2) SHARDA CONSTRUCTIONS LLP, a limited liability partnership registered under
the Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008 (LLPIN AAA-1111), having its
registered office at 14, Industrial Estate, Andheri East, Mumbai 400093 (the
"Contractor").

WHEREAS:

(A) The Employer is the owner of Meridian House, Mumbai, and has agreed to
grant a fit-out licence in respect of Unit 401 of the Building to Acme
Analytics Private Limited ("Tenant A");

(B) The Contractor has represented that it is experienced in office fit-out
works and has agreed to perform the Works on the terms of this Contract.

NOW IT IS HEREBY AGREED as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION
1.1 "Contract Price" means Rs. 2,40,00,000 (Rupees two crore and forty lakh
    only), inclusive of GST.
1.2 "Completion Date" means 30 June 2026, as extended (if at all) under
    Clause 5.
1.3 "Works" means the interior fit-out of Unit 401 (8,000 sq. ft.) described
    in Schedule 1 (Scope of Works) and the Drawings.
1.4 Words and expressions not defined here bear the meaning given in the
    Indian Contract Act, 1872.

2. SCOPE AND COMMENCEMENT
2.1 The Contractor shall perform and complete the Works with due diligence
    and in a good and workmanlike manner.
2.2 The Works shall commence on 20 January 2026 ("Commencement Date").

3. EMPLOYER'S OBLIGATIONS
3.1 The Employer shall provide access to Unit 401 from the Commencement Date
    during working hours and shall obtain the building association's no
    objection for the Works.

4. CONTRACT PRICE AND PAYMENT
4.1 The Employer shall pay the Contract Price against Running Bills certified
    by the Employer's architect within 30 (thirty) days of certification.
4.2 Retention Money of 5% (five per cent) of each Running Bill shall be
    withheld until the Defects Liability Period expires.

5. DELAY AND EXTENSION OF TIME
5.1 If the Works are not completed by the Completion Date, the Contractor
    shall pay or the Employer may deduct, as liquidated damages, a rate of
    0.5% (zero point five per cent) of the Contract Price per completed week
    of delay, subject to an aggregate cap of 5% (five per cent) of the
    Contract Price.
5.2 The Contractor shall be entitled to a fair extension of the Completion
    Date for delays not attributable to the Contractor, notified within
    14 (fourteen) days of the commencement of such delay.

6. DEFECTS LIABILITY
6.1 The Contractor shall remedy defects notified within 12 (twelve) months
    of the date of completion ("Defects Liability Period").

7. TERMINATION
7.1 The Employer may terminate for contractor's default on 14 (fourteen)
    days' notice specifying the default, if the default is not remedied.

8. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
8.1 Any dispute shall be referred to arbitration of a sole arbitrator
    appointed under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996; the seat of
    arbitration shall be Mumbai; the language shall be English.

9. GOVERNING LAW
9.1 This Contract is governed by the laws of India; courts at Mumbai have
    exclusive jurisdiction.

SIGNED for and on behalf of the Employer: ______________ (Director)
SIGNED for and on behalf of the Contractor: ______________ (Designated Partner)
